One sitting. A plan you can build from.

We work through your idea one question at a time. You talk, we write it down and push back where it's vague. At the end you have six things.

  1. A product brief

    The problem, who has it, what success looks like and what's out of scope, written so anyone can follow it.

  2. The competition, checked

    Who else solves this, what they charge and what their customers complain about. Every claim links to its source.

  3. A way to make money

    Who pays, a model that fits, and a starting price to test.

  4. Two or three design directions

    Genuinely different looks, each drawn on your app's most important screen with your real content.

  5. A deck to show people

    Eleven slides you can put in front of a partner, a boss or an investor.

  6. A build list

    Every feature sorted into now, next and later, with what "done" means for each. Ready for us, or anyone, to build.

Who you'll be working with

Pivot Dev Shop is Braden Turner. Braden is a product leader: the person on a team who works out what's worth building, who it's for and what to leave out. Pivot is that job done from start to finish. The thinking, the design and the build happen in one place, with no hand-off in the middle where good ideas get lost.

Building with AI changes the pace. Garage went from a prototype to a working app in a long weekend, so you see the real thing early and react to it, instead of approving a slide about it.

Questions people ask first

Who is Pivot Dev Shop for?
People with an idea and no product team behind it yet: founders, small business owners, and teams inside bigger companies who need something real to show. You don't need to be technical.
What happens in the first session?
We work through your idea together: the problem, the goal, who it's for, the competition, how it makes money, how it should look and what to build first. Then we write it all up. It takes about 75 minutes, and you can stop after any step and pick it up another day.
What do I get at the end?
A product brief, two or three design directions drawn on your app's main screen, a short deck you can show partners or investors, and a build list sorted into now, next and later.
What if my idea isn't a good one?
Then we'll say so. If research shows someone already solves the problem well, we'll show you, and talk through whether to narrow the idea, change it or go ahead knowing that.
Do you build it too?
Yes. The same person who planned it designs and builds it, so nothing gets lost between steps. If you'd rather hand the plan to your own developers, it's written so they can pick it up.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the idea, so we quote once we understand it. Telling us about it costs nothing.

Got an idea you keep coming back to?

Tell us what it is and what's stuck. We'll tell you honestly whether we're the right shop for it.
